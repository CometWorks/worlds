# Octahedron logo

The station silhouette is flanked by the CometWorks comet in the upper left
and a stylized double-helix Moon shipyard in the upper right. The corner marks
do not overlap the station, and the artwork fits a circular avatar crop.

- `octahedron.svg`: editable master, with no fonts or external image dependencies.
- `octahedron-{size}.png`: square icons at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 pixels.
- `octahedron.ico`: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256-pixel images in one file.
- `preview.png`: square server-list icons and circular avatar previews.

Use the 1024 or 512-pixel PNG for uploads to Discord or Quasar.

The station geometry comes from `../blueprints/octahedron-station.sbc`.
The comet was redrawn from the CometWorks website's
`assets/brand/cometworks-mark.png`, retaining its round head and three tails.
The helix represents the shipyard generated by `../make_moon_shipyard.py`.

PNG exports were rendered with Inkscape on Linux. The ICO contains the matching
PNG exports, packed with Pillow. To render a PNG from this folder:

```sh
inkscape octahedron.svg --export-width=512 --export-filename=octahedron-512.png
```
