Welcome to Octahedron! The server is in beta testing.

Free ship: at a station's shipyard, load a blueprint into the hologram table, check the parts list on the billboard and press the button next to it. A ship is free while you have less than 60,000 PCU, one every 10 minutes.

Trade lanes: stop just in front of a tunnel's entrance ring and turn off your dampeners (Z). The gate takes your ship to the far end. Dampeners back on cancels the trip.

Factions: open the terminal (K), Factions tab, and join one. Anyone is accepted.
- Sheep [SHP]: We prefer peaceful gameplay and defense. We want to socialize and build together.
- Wolves [WOL]: We are warriors and great hunters. We want to train, hunt, salvage and occasionally play with the sheep.
Sheep and Wolves are enemies. You can also make your own faction and send peace to both; they accept it, and you are neutral to everyone in them. Without a faction, everybody shows up as an enemy.

Limits: 60,000 PCU per ship or station, 1,000,000 PCU per player.
World: 500 km radius from the planet's center. Ships slow down past 450 km.

Rules:
1. No bigotry, slurs or similar in chat, faction names and grids.
2. No harassment. Keep everything safe for work.
3. No gravity drives, piston guns or other lag generators.
4. Don't back-seat moderate. Bring problems with other players to the admins.
5. Have fun, and don't take it too seriously.

Programmable block scripts need the Scripter role, which admins grant.
