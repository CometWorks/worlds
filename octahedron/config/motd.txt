Welcome to Octahedron! The server is in beta testing.

Free ship: at a station's shipyard, load a blueprint into the hologram table, read the quote on its screen and press the button next to it. A ship is free while you have less than 10,000 PCU, one per hour.

Limits: 60,000 PCU per ship or station, 1,000,000 PCU per player.
World: 500 km radius from the planet's center. Ships slow down past 450 km.

Rules:
1. No bigotry, slurs or similar in chat, faction names and grids.
2. No harassment. Keep everything safe for work.
3. No gravity drives, piston guns or other lag generators.
4. Don't back-seat moderate. Bring problems with other players to the admins.
5. Have fun, and don't take it too seriously.

Programmable block scripts need the Scripter role, which admins grant.
