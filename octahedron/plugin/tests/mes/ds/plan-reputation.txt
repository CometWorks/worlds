# SE-1294: THIE's reputation trigger on an Atlas cargo ship. A and B are in OCT, C and D in TST.
# A rams the Atlas with one grid, then A and C each hit it from grids further away with 30 bullets 100 ms apart.
rep-setup
wait 3
rep-join
rep-spawn SpaceCargoShip THIE-202-AS-Atlas 100000 0 0
wait 30
rep-target Atlas
rep-grid ram A 40
rep-grid gunA A 1000
rep-grid gunC C 1500
wait 3
rep start
rep-ram ram 8 25
wait 12
rep rammed
rep-hit gunA Bullet 30 100
wait 5
rep shot-A
rep-hit gunC Bullet 30 100
wait 5
rep shot-C
done
