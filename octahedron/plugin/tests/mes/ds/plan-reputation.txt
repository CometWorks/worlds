# SE-1294: THIE's reputation trigger on an Atlas cargo ship. A and B are in OCT, C and D in TST.
# A rams the Atlas and then hits it from another grid with 30 bullets 100 ms apart; then C does the same
# from the other side.
rep-setup
wait 3
rep-join
rep-spawn SpaceCargoShip THIE-202-AS-Atlas 100000 0 0
wait 30
rep-target Atlas
rep-grid ramA A 40
rep-grid gunA A 1000
rep-grid gunC C 1500
wait 3
rep start
rep-ram ramA 8 25
wait 12
rep rammed-A
rep-hit gunA Bullet 30 100
wait 5
rep shot-A
rep-grid ramC C -40
wait 2
rep-ram ramC 8 25
wait 12
rep rammed-C
rep-hit gunC Bullet 30 100
wait 5
rep shot-C
done
